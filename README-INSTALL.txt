BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Replace https://YOUR-WEBSITE-DOMAIN in all canonical tags, robots.txt and sitemap.xml with your final website URL.
2. Confirm every service area mentioned is genuinely served. Remove areas you do not serve.
3. Add genuine photos of your actual fleet and handover process.
4. Add exact models, prices, deposits, document requirements, insurance/excess, fuel and kilometre policies only after confirming them.
5. Add your real public business address and hours only if you want customers to visit and those details are accurate.
6. Forms open WhatsApp and send an enquiry. They do not check live availability, accept payments or confirm bookings.

FREE HOSTING
Upload the extracted files to a static host such as GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages. Check current platform terms and limits for commercial use.

SEO SETUP
- Connect and verify the website in Google Search Console.
- Replace the placeholder domain, then submit /sitemap.xml.
- Use URL Inspection to request indexing for key pages.
- Keep business name, phone and address consistent with the real Business Profile.
- Use genuine local photos and useful, original service information.
- Ask real customers for honest reviews; never buy or fabricate reviews.
- Search ranking is not guaranteed.

Google's SEO guidance recommends clear unique page titles and descriptions, useful original content, descriptive links and a sitemap to help discovery.
